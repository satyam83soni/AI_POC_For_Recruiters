# Skillpath Certificates

## Earning a certificate

A certificate is issued when a learner completes 100% of the required lessons and scores at least 70% on the final assessment. Optional lessons do not count toward completion.

The final assessment can be attempted 3 times. After the third failed attempt there is a 14-day cooldown before the attempts reset.

## Verification

Every certificate has a unique verification ID in the format SKP-YYYY-XXXXXX. Employers can verify a certificate at skillpath.example/verify by entering that ID. Verification pages stay live for 10 years from the issue date.

## Name changes

The name on a certificate is taken from the learner's profile at the moment of issue. To correct it, the learner raises a ticket with a government ID attached. Corrections are processed within 3 business days, and the verification ID does not change.

## LinkedIn

Certificates can be added to LinkedIn directly from the certificate page using the Add to Profile button. The credential URL points to the verification page.
