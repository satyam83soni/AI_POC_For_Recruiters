# Skillpath AI Tutor

## What it does

The AI tutor answers questions about the lesson a learner is currently watching. It retrieves passages from the course transcript and notes, and answers only from those passages. When the course material does not cover a question, the tutor says so instead of guessing.

## Limits

Free plan learners get 1 question per day. Pro and Campus learners have a fair-use limit of 200 questions per day. Each answer is capped at roughly 400 words.

## Languages

The tutor understands questions in English, Hindi, and Hinglish, and replies in the language the question was asked in. Course transcripts are indexed in English only.

## Data handling

Tutor conversations are stored for 90 days to improve answer quality, then deleted. Learners can delete their tutor history at any time from Account Settings, then Privacy. Conversations are never used to train third-party models.

## Known limitations

The tutor cannot see code a learner has written in an external editor. It cannot grade assessments or change scores.
