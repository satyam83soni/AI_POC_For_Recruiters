# Skillpath Refund Policy

## Eligibility

Learners can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided they have watched less than 30% of the course videos. After 7 days, or once 30% or more of the content has been watched, the purchase is non-refundable.

Bundles are treated as one purchase. Watching 30% of any single course in a bundle counts as 30% of the bundle.

## How to request a refund

Refunds are requested from Account Settings, then Purchases, then Request Refund. Support agents cannot issue refunds directly; they can only escalate a ticket to the Billing team.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. UPI refunds usually arrive within 48 hours. Refunds to international cards can take up to 14 business days.

## Exceptions

Learners on the Campus plan (purchased by a college on their behalf) cannot request refunds individually. The college administrator must raise the request.

Courses bought with promotional credits are refunded as credits, never as cash.
