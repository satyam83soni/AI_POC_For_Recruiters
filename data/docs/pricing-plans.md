# Skillpath Pricing Plans

## Free

The Free plan includes the first 3 lessons of every course, community forum access, and one AI tutor question per day. No certificates are issued on the Free plan.

## Pro

Pro costs Rs 799 per month or Rs 6,999 per year. It includes every course, unlimited AI tutor questions (subject to a fair-use limit of 200 per day), certificates, and offline downloads on up to 2 devices.

## Campus

Campus is sold to colleges, priced per seat with a minimum of 100 seats. Pricing starts at Rs 2,400 per seat per year. Campus includes everything in Pro plus an admin dashboard, cohort analytics, and placement-readiness reports.

## Plan changes

Upgrading from Free to Pro takes effect immediately. Downgrading from Pro to Free takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle; downloaded content is removed from devices at that point.

Switching from monthly to yearly billing gives a prorated credit for the unused part of the current month.
